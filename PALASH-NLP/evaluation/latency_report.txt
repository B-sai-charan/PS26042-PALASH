LATENCY INTERPRETATION
============================================================

The latency measurements in this report were obtained on the
NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 Laptop GPU development machine.

The first valid translation invocation can include model and
GPU warm-up overhead and may therefore be significantly slower
than subsequent inference calls.

For the five tested FLN sentences, observed inference latency
ranged from 219.51 ms to 422.87 ms, with an average of 298.69 ms.

These are model inference measurements on the development GPU.

They are NOT end-to-end PALASH application latency measurements.

They do NOT establish performance on an Android device.

They do NOT establish offline Android inference.

They do NOT establish a guaranteed translation latency of
0.31 seconds for all PALASH translations.

The previously observed approximately 0.31-second figure should
therefore be described only as an observed steady-state inference
measurement under the tested development-GPU conditions.
