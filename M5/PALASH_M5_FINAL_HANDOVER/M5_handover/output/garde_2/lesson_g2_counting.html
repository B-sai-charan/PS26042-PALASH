PALASH Grade 2 Mathematics - Counting and Numbers

Grade 2 | Mathematics | Counting Numbers And Operations

Learning Objective: Students identify, count, read and match numbers from 1 to 10 using objects and bilingual Hindi-Santali support.

Expected Learning Outcome: Students can recognize numbers 1-10, count simple groups of objects, and connect numerals with Hindi and Santali number words.
